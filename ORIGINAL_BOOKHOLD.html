About Us ×

BOOKHOLD is a digital library reservation platform designed to help users browse, reserve, and download books with ease.

It combines a clean library-inspired interface with simple features such as book discovery, reservation tracking, downloads, wishlist management, and category browsing.

The goal of BOOKHOLD is to make reading more organized, accessible, and enjoyable for students and readers.

FAQs ×
How do I reserve a book?

Open Discover Books or any genre page, then click Reserve.

How do I download a book?

You can download from Reservations or use Read Now from book cards.

Why do books appear under Completed?

Books move to Completed once you finish reading or reach full progress.

Why do books appear under Expired?

Expired means you removed, cancelled, or the item is no longer active.
